        08 · Questions

        What general managers ask first.

        We already run guest surveys. What is different?

        Surveys measure satisfaction with what you asked about. Lens reads what guests volunteer, unprompted, in public: which sensations, people and moments they carry away, and which parts of your promise never appear in their language.

        Why is there no free strategy call?

        Because the work is designed to run without taking your time. You can buy on the scope alone, and the 60-minute debrief is included. If you want a separate conversation with the founder, the Personal Call exists for exactly that.

        What if the public evidence is thin?

        The report says so, names the limitation and tells you which decision should wait. We do not fill gaps with assumptions.

        Will this lead to coverage in Escale Privée?

        No. Belonging Lens is a separate practice of Arcan Schalier Group. Buying it has no effect on editorial decisions, and no commercial client is promised, or denied, coverage.
